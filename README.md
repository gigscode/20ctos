# 20 Conversations. One Purpose: Progress.

Sometimes a helpful conversation is a good place to start.

I am Olajide Igbalaye. I build digital products, teach technology, explore practical uses for AI, and work with people who are trying to move forward.

I am making room for 20 conversations with people who are building a business, working through an idea, growing their skills, creating for a living, or looking for a little direction.

## What we can talk about

Bring the thing on your mind. It might be a business problem, a project you want to start, a career choice, a new skill, or a challenge you have not quite found your way through.

There is no pitch and no need to have everything figured out. The aim is to listen, share ideas, and see if there is a useful next step.

## Who may find this helpful

- People running or starting a business
- People with an idea they want to make real
- People thinking about work, skills, or a new opportunity
- Creators, freelancers, and independent professionals
- Anyone who would value a thoughtful conversation

## What happens next

Tell me a little about what is going on and what you hope to get from a conversation. Your details are meant to help arrange that conversation and follow up about what you shared. They are not for marketing messages.
